The Model

The pitch line, turn a $4B company into a $10B one, is the most delicate sentence in the venture, so it was built like a case, not claimed like a boast. A first value model went to a six-model counsel panel, came back with ambition intact and six unanimous fixes, and was rebuilt from the $10B identity backward into sourced components. Every number carries a MEASURED, SURVEY, or VENDOR lineage chip; the ghost 30 to 40% stat was excised and the Dimon $2B figure was secured to a real source. The design principle: the distribution carries the aggression, so the sensitivity table does the arguing, not a single point estimate.
