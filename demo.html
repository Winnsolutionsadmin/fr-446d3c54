05 · The one thing that needs you

The fleet runs. The human decides.

Money, anything irreversible, and anything a person outside the company could see: none of it happens without a human. Here is what is waiting. In this room the controls are inert on purpose.

How autonomy is earned

An operator earns the right to act alone on a decision class only after it matches the human's own choice on at least ninety percent of twenty or more decisions in that class. Until then, every one of these is a card, not an action.
